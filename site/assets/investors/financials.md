## The deals, October 2026

AMTECH is a platform for creating, running and managing AI employees, each a team of AI agents with a computer of its own. The platform's first line of code was written on August 24, 2026. Four deals were signed in October, each on its own terms. The price shape is a setup fee, then usage; two deals add a share of what the business earns.

| Business | Terms | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A Ventura County service company | 8% of all its revenue. The employee does everything except the physical work. | Signed |
| A Houston moving company | $1,200 setup, then usage. Being set up to run the whole business. | Signed |
| A Pennsylvania festival organizer | $1,200 for the employee, plus 10% of all the money the festival raises. | In use since Oct 8 |
| A crypto trading desk | $1,200 setup, then usage. | Signed |

Clients are named by kind until each gives permission. Before the platform, the founder built websites and systems for paying clients in Pennsylvania, California and Texas; that was freelance work and is not counted as AMTECH revenue. AMTECH has run its own business on one of its employees since August.

## What it costs to run an employee, measured

Every model call runs through AMTECH's own gateway, which meters cost and price per call. Cost is read from that ledger (7,428 calls to 2026-10-17): typical employee work costs about 5 cents an hour in model calls; the busiest continuous stretch on record cost 46 cents an hour. The basis below is an employee working 3 hours a day, 5 days a week, with a few scheduled jobs.

| Per employee, per month | Typical | Worst case |
|---|---|---|
| Working time, 65 hours of model calls | $3.25 | $29.90 |
| Scheduled jobs, about 77 runs | $1.00 | $3.85 |
| Pictures and video allowance | $5.00 | $10.00 |
| Its computer, storage and backups | $5.00 | $5.00 |
| Phone number, texts and email | $3.00 | $3.00 |
| **Cost to serve** | **about $17** | **about $52** |

Usage is billed today at about an 85% margin on that cost. A flat price of 50 cents per working hour is in test; at that price the 65 hours above bill $32.50 a month. The price of a fixed level of AI capability has been falling 9 to 900 times a year (Epoch AI), so the cost line falls with every model release. AMTECH is in price discovery on purpose: setup plus usage, a flat hourly price, and revenue share run side by side, and the gateway measures each one.

## What the $600,000 does

It pays for the computers and the AI the employees run on, the free trials, getting owners to the page, and the company's running costs, with a reserve. The founder does the meetings and the go-to-market himself with AI agents, so none of it goes to hiring. At the end of eighteen months any owner who finds a page can sign up without him.
