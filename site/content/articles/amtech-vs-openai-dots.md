## What Dots are

Dots, announced by OpenAI on 29 September 2026, are always-on agents inside ChatGPT. Each dot has its own cloud computer, keeps working between conversations, connects to apps through ChatGPT, and can be reached in ChatGPT, Slack and Microsoft Teams. Custom rules can allow an action, ask first, or forbid it, and an activity view shows what the dot is doing. At launch each eligible user gets one dot, and OpenAI is piloting specialist Dots for business roles with enterprise customers.

It is a capable product. The difference is who it is made for and who does the setting up.

If your team already lives in ChatGPT and each person wants an agent that keeps working on their own tasks, a dot fits well.
