## What Grok Bot is

Grok Bot is xAI's AI teammate, run on Cursor's cloud. Each Bot has a name, a job and its own conversation, and all of a person's Bots share one persistent cloud computer with a browser, a command line and files. Bots connect to apps such as Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Slack and Notion, run routines on a schedule or an event, and can hand work to each other. It is included with paid X, SuperGrok and Cursor plans, and teams can share Bots.

It is a capable product. The difference is who it is made for and who does the setting up.

## Side by side

| | Grok Bot | AMTECH |
|---|---|---|
| **Made for** | One person and the Bots they create | A business, with a team of agents built for its work |
| **Who builds the agents** | You create each Bot, connect apps and write routines | A lead agent reads the business and builds the team; on a custom setup, AMTECH builds it with you |
| **Whose account** | The person's account; the computer "belongs to your account" | The business's account, with a team for each business, store or client |
| **Connections** | Apps act as the connected person's own account; Gmail connects one mailbox at a time | Each agent gets its own sign-in to the business's tools, and works through the screen where there is no other way in |
| **Usage** | A weekly pool shared with Cursor and the Grok apps; pauses until reset when on-demand is off | Pay as you go per job, or a monthly custom engagement sized to the business |
| **Results** | Yours to set and check | On a custom setup, the results are agreed up front and guaranteed |
| **Several businesses or clients** | Bots can be shared with a team; no per-business setup is documented | One screen across every business, store or client |

## When Grok Bot is the right choice

If you want a personal assistant for your own research, decks, inbox and calendar, and you enjoy setting up and tuning your own Bots, Grok Bot is a good tool, and many owners will keep one.

## When AMTECH is the right choice

If you want parts of the business to run every day, such as finding and contacting prospects, keeping product listings and ads current across stores, or getting invoices out and paid, you need agents built around the business, connected to its software, with rules, records and costs the owner can see.
