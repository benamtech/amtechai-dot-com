questions:
  - q: "Is the ChatGPT business directory free?"
    a: "Yes. Claiming and keeping a page costs nothing, and a free ChatGPT account works. OpenAI hasn't said whether it will ever charge, so it's worth claiming yours while it's simply free."
  - q: "Is it the same as a Google Business Profile?"
    a: "It's the same idea in a different place. Your Google profile is what Google shows, and your ChatGPT page is what ChatGPT shows. Keep both, with the same name, phone, hours and services, so neither one contradicts the other."
  - q: "I can't find my business in Business Hub. What now?"
    a: "Local businesses can only claim a listing ChatGPT already has. If yours isn't there, ChatGPT doesn't know enough about you yet, and that's the same reason it isn't recommending you. Getting onto the lists and directories it reads is the fix. [Run the free check](#check) and we'll show you which ones."
  - q: "Can an agency claim my business for me?"
    a: "No, and you shouldn't let one try. You claim it with your own account and do the ID check yourself, because it needs your ID. Then you add the agency from inside Business Hub. We do the claim with you on a 20-minute call."
  - q: "Someone else already claimed my business. Can I fix that?"
    a: "Yes. Open the listing, choose Dispute ownership from the menu, and verify with Persona. If the listing is locked, it shows Contact support."
  - q: "How long does approval take?"
    a: "The claim itself takes about 15 minutes. OpenAI reviews it and emails you, and changes to your page are reviewed before they appear, so allow a few days for everything to show."
  - q: "Can I delete my page later?"
    a: "A draft can be deleted. Once a page is published, OpenAI support can't unpublish or hide it, so publish with care and keep it current instead."
  - q: "Does claiming my page make ChatGPT recommend me?"
    a: "Not by itself. It makes what ChatGPT shows about you correct and gives you your numbers. Being named as one of the best comes from the lists, reviews and pages ChatGPT reads, and that's the rest of what we do."
