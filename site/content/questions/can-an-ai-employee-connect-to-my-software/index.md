questions_h: What owners ask next
questions:
- q: What if my software is not one you have heard of?
  a: It still connects. Builder, the teammate that makes software, builds the connection where none exists. Anything that sends an email, has a sign-in, or posts a notice when something changes can reach
    your employee, and where a system has none of those, it works from the exports you already make.
- q: Can it replace the software I already pay for?
  a: Yes. On day one it plugs into what you run so nothing changes for your crews, and from there it can do the whole job of a scheduling, quoting or follow-up subscription itself, with a custom setup built
    around how your business works. You decide which subscriptions to cancel.
- q: Can I take its access away?
  a: Yes. Each seat your employee holds is a user you can see in that system's own settings, and removing it there ends its access at once.
- q: Do I need someone technical to set it up?
  a: No. You describe your business in words and approve the screens that ask for your consent, and your employee does the rest and tells you when each connection works.
