steps_h: How every lead gets followed up
steps:
- h: A lead arrives
  p: A form on your site, a message on your Google listing or an email to the shop reaches your employee, which reads what the customer asked for and where they are.
- h: The first reply goes out fast
  p: Follow-up answers from your prices and your calendar, with a real time to come and look or a price range, while the customer is still choosing who to call.
- h: You approve it, once or for good
  p: You say yes to each reply, or give a standing approval in your own words, such as first replies to anyone asking for a gutter cleaning quote.
- h: It follows up until there is an answer
  p: Two days later, then a week later, Follow-up sends the next short note, changes the offer only when you tell it to, and stops the moment the customer says yes or no.

  p: When the customer says yes, Collections sends the deposit request and your employee puts the visit on your calendar, so the lead you won does not go quiet a second time.
